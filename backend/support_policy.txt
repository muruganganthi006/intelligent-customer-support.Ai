IntelliSupport AI - Customer Support Policy

1. Ticket Creation
Customers can create support tickets by providing a subject, description, and priority.

2. Ticket Priority
Low priority: General questions and minor issues.
Medium priority: Issues affecting normal application usage.
High priority: Critical problems that prevent customers from using the application.

3. Ticket Status
Open: A newly created support ticket.
In Progress: The support team is working on the issue.
Resolved: A solution has been provided.
Closed: The support request has been completed.

4. Support Response
The support team reviews submitted tickets and provides updates through the application.

5. Account Assistance
Customers should contact the support team if they experience account access problems.
